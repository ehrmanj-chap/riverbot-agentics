About

Independent, experimental, and deliberately small.

Riverbot Agentics is an independent applied AI consulting practice led by Jordan Ehrman. We work on agentic systems, AI research prototypes, educational tools, and the messy middle between an interesting model behavior and a dependable real-world workflow.

Engagements can range from a technical review or prototype sprint to ongoing research collaboration, curriculum development, agent evaluation, and system design.

Riverbot Agentics is operated by STEMcell LLC.
